Audiogravity comes in two editions — a free Starter Edition and a Pro License — and every install starts with a 30-day trial of Pro.

Trial — 30 days

Full access to every feature, automatically activated on first run. No action required. Days remaining are shown in Admin › License.

Starter Edition — Free

Activated automatically when the trial expires — no action required. It keeps receiving the updates of its major version (e.g. v1.x), as Pro does. Includes the essentials to run your audio system and play the music stored on it:

  • Profiles — one-click switching between pre-configured audio chain scenarios; activates required services and stops conflicting ones automatically
  • Services — real-time monitoring and control of audio services (start / stop / restart / enable at boot) with live CPU, memory and I/O metrics, each with a sparkline of its recent values
  • Software — install, update and remove audio packages, with a simulation before committing changes
  • System — hardware dashboard: CPU, temperature, memory, disk and network at a glance; full audio device inventory (ALSA cards, USB interfaces, subdevices)
  • Users — role-based access management (Admin, User, Guest) with WebAuthn / passkeys login
  • Push notifications — iOS, Android and desktop alerts when a service goes down, the processor overheats, the disk is nearly full, a Raspberry Pi's power supply is too weak, a new version of Audiogravity or an update of an audio package is available, a profile is activated, or a USB drive is plugged in or pulled out — with whether the music library on it is ready
  • One-click updates — Audiogravity updates itself from the Admin page, core and interface together, and goes back to the previous version on its own if anything goes wrong (administrators)
  • Audio Services Config — safe in-place editing of each audio service's own configuration file, with a diff before saving, a basic syntax check and automatic backups (HQPlayer Embedded's, the NAA's and Roon's come with Pro)
  • Guided provisioning — generate a minimal, correct configuration for the whole stack (output, music library, network mounts) in a few clicks, instead of editing each service by hand (administrators only)
  • DSD protection — your DAC's own volume set to maximum while it receives DSD, then put back; switchable for a DAC that lowers DSD properly
  • Library — the music stored on the box: album browsing, full-text search and queue management
  • Internet radio — Radio Browser directory, custom stations, favourites, and a filter that keeps the stations that really stream lossless
  • Player — play/pause, next/prev and volume for the box's music and the radio, in the mini-player and the full-screen player; seek, repeat/shuffle and the sleep timer in the full-screen player
  • Outputs — the box's DAC (USB, Toslink, HDMI…), switched without touching the streamer, or a DLNA/UPnP network speaker (UPnP Control Point)
  • AirPlay and casting — the box also plays what an iPhone, iPad or Mac sends it over AirPlay, or what a UPnP/DLNA app casts to it, and shows it in Now Playing
  • Now Playing — current track, cover art, format and progress across all sources

What Pro adds stays in view on a Starter box, behind a padlock: a tap opens the licence panel. When the trial ends, streaming tracks still in the queue no longer play; the music on the box plays on.

Pro License — one-time payment, no subscription

Permanently unlocks all features. Bound to one installation on one machine; moving to another machine takes a licence reset, available on request. Includes all updates within the purchased major version (e.g. v1.x). Existing holders receive a preferential upgrade price for new major versions.

Everything in Starter, plus:

  • Streaming — Qobuz, Tidal and HIGHRESAUDIO: album browsing, full-text search, queue, favourites and playlists
  • UPnP media servers — MinimServer and the other servers of your network, browsed and searched like the box's own library
  • Roon — your Roon library, its zones and their transport controls
  • HQPlayer — HQPlayer as the output, and its DSP remote: filter, noise shaper, output mode and volume from the couch, with automatic network discovery; HQPlayer Embedded's and the NAA's output and settings
  • Listening history — what the box played: time listened per day or month, sources, outputs, sound quality, genres, and the most played artists, albums and tracks; kept on the box, recorded on every edition and switched off or cleared from any of them
  • VU meters — two needles that follow what the box's own player plays, in step with the DAC: in the cover's place in the full-screen player, or over the whole screen in showcase mode
  • Audio Pipeline — interactive DAG visualisation of the full signal chain from source to DAC: full-resolution badge on the link to the DAC, format per link, real-time output steering without stopping playback
  • Systemd Configuration — fine-tune audio service drop-ins: CPU affinity, SCHED_FIFO / RR priority, MEMLOCK and RTPRIO — so the audio services keep the processor when the box is busy
  • Performance Optimization — per-core CPU governor control, real-time thermal throttle detection, µs-scale latency benchmarks (cyclictest), network tests (ping, iPerf3) and live RT process monitor

Qobuz, Tidal and HIGHRESAUDIO require an active subscription to their respective services. Platform: Debian 13 (Trixie) or later — DietPi and Raspberry Pi OS included, in their Trixie release — on x86-64 and Raspberry Pi (aarch64). The core installer turns any other system down.

How to Purchase

  1. Click Pay with PayPal in Admin › License.
  2. You will receive your license key by email within seconds.
  3. Click License Key, enter your key and activate this machine.
  4. All features are immediately available — no restart required.
