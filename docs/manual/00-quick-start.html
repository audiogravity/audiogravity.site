Quick start — from zero to music

The whole journey on one page. Every step links to its full chapter when you want the detail.

1. Install Audiogravity on the box

curl -fsSL https://audiogravity.app/install.sh | sudo bash -s -- --token ghp_xxx

One command on your DietPi / Debian box — core and interface together, keys generated, services started. (Early-access token required — see 2. Installation.)

2. Open it and sign in

Browse to https://<ip-of-your-box>. Your 30-day trial activates by itself. Sign in as admin / admin123 — and change that password right away (Admin tab → your card). (Details and passkeys: 3. First run.)

3. Install the audio engines

Audio Software tab → INSTALL on Music Player Daemon (the core player), plus Shairport Sync (AirPlay) and UPnP Bridge if you want them.

4. Initialize the audio stack

Config tab → Initialize audio stack: pick your DAC and your music library — a USB drive, or a NAS share added right from the picker (mounted and tested on the spot) — and confirm with your admin password. The generated configs are minimal and bit-perfect.

5. Connect your music

  • Local library — already there: whatever you picked in step 4.
  • Qobuz, Tidal, HIGHRESAUDIO — Library → Sources, sign in to each service with your own subscription (walkthroughs: 5. Library & streaming).
  • Internet radio — works out of the box, starter stations included.

6. Play

Library tab → pick an album → it plays on your DAC. Tap the Now Playing bar for the fullscreen player and the live hi-fi readout — format, sample rate, bitrate (tour: 4. Listening).

7. Make it yours (optional, five more minutes)

  • Add it to your phone's home screen — fullscreen app, portrait lock.
  • Register a passkey — Face ID / Touch ID login (needs HTTPS).
  • Send music elsewhere — network renderers, HQPlayer, AirPlay.
  • Tune the OS for audio (Pro) — real-time scheduling, CPU pinning, latency tests.
