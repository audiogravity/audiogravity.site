# Guides

Answers to the questions audiophiles ask about Raspberry Pi and Linux streamers — DACs, libraries,
streaming, Roon, HQPlayer, DSD — checked and dated.

- [A DAC HAT on a Raspberry Pi: why it is not detected, and the line that fixes it](dac-hat-raspberry-pi.md) — Why a HiFiBerry or other DAC board on a Raspberry Pi is not detected, the config.txt line for each common board, and how to check it is seen.
- [Your NAS music on a Raspberry Pi streamer: mount the share, or use a UPnP server](nas-music-library-raspberry-pi.md) — Two ways to play the music on your NAS from a Pi streamer — mount the share, or use a UPnP server such as MinimServer — and how Audiogravi<sup>ty</sup> does both.
- [Qobuz, Tidal and HIGHRESAUDIO on a Raspberry Pi](qobuz-tidal-highresaudio-raspberry-pi.md) — The ways to play Qobuz, Tidal and HIGHRESAUDIO on a Pi — Volumio, Lyrion, moOde, HQPlayer — what each costs and offers, and how Audiogravi<sup>ty</sup> does it.
- [Roon on a Raspberry Pi: Roon Bridge, and Roon through HQPlayer](roon-raspberry-pi.md) — Why a Pi is a Roon endpoint and not a Core, the ways to set one up, how to listen to Roon through HQPlayer and its NAA, and what Audiogravi<sup>ty</sup> does.
- [HQPlayer Embedded on a Raspberry Pi 5](hqplayer-embedded-raspberry-pi-5.md) — Two ways to install HQPlayer Embedded on a Pi 5, what the board can handle, and how Audiogravi<sup>ty</sup> sets it up next to your other players.
- [HQPlayer NAA on a Raspberry Pi](hqplayer-naa-raspberry-pi.md) — What an NAA does, the two ways to install it on a Pi, the version rule, Pi 4 or Pi 5 for a USB DAC, and how Audiogravi<sup>ty</sup> sets it up.
- [DSD on a Linux streamer: native, DoP, or converted to PCM](dsd-native-dop-linux.md) — The three ways DSD reaches a DAC on Linux — native, DoP, converted to PCM — what decides between them, how to check yours, and what Audiogravi<sup>ty</sup> does.
- [How to check bit-perfect playback on a Linux streamer](check-bit-perfect-playback-linux.md) — Three checks to see whether a Linux streamer sends your files to the DAC untouched, what MPD's software volume does, and what Audiogravi<sup>ty</sup> shows.
- [Real-time priority and reserved CPU cores for audio on Linux](realtime-cpu-cores-audio-linux.md) — Real-time priority, pinned and reserved CPU cores for audio on Linux: what each does, what it changes in measured latency, and what Audiogravi<sup>ty</sup> does.
- [“Device or resource busy”: two players, one DAC](device-or-resource-busy-dac.md) — The error message two players get when they share one DAC on Linux, how to find which program holds it, when each lets go, and what to do instead.
- [Audiogravi<sup>ty</sup>, Volumio or moOde: which to choose?](audiogravity-volumio-moode.md) — A sourced, dated comparison by the maker of Audiogravi<sup>ty</sup>: price, streaming services, Qobuz and TIDAL Connect, HQPlayer, Roon, DSD and apps.
