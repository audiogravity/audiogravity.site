Audiogravity · Halloween 2026

The orange ghost: the rules

The game

On 31 October 2026, ten orange ghosts haunt audiogravity.app. Each one is a lifetime Audiogravity licence for whoever catches it.

Who runs it

Alexandre Di Marco, publisher of Audiogravity at audiogravity.app. For anything about the game: orange-ghost@audiogravity.app.

When

From midnight to midnight on 31 October 2026, Paris time (UTC+1). The page shows it on 31 October by your own clock, so elsewhere you can play while both days meet: in New York from midnight to 19:00, in Tokyo from 08:00 to midnight.

How it works

  1. Through the day, the ten licences wake one at a time, each at a moment drawn at random and kept secret by the Audiogravity licence server: one moment in each tenth of the day.
  2. The first visitor to open audiogravity.app after a licence wakes is shown an orange ghost, somewhere on the page. Nobody else sees it.
  3. Catch it, with a click or a tap, within twenty minutes, and the page gives you the licence key. If you do not, it goes back to sleep, and the next visitor has their chance.

Through the day, the banner at the top of the page says how many licences are left to win. Those nobody has caught by midnight are not awarded.

The prize

A lifetime licence, the same as a bought one: Audiogravity Pro on one installation, every 1.x version included, with no end date. It has no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

Using your key

In Audiogravity, open the Admin tab, tap LICENSE KEY at the top of the licence panel and paste the key, then tap CHECK KEY and ACTIVATE THIS MACHINE. The key works once: the first box to activate it keeps it, so keep it to yourself. It does not expire: with no box yet, it will wait for you. The page you caught it on keeps it on that device.

Who can play, and how often

Anyone, free of charge: no purchase, no account, no email address. Each internet address has one chance an hour, and can win one licence.

Fair play

Asking with automated requests, or through many addresses to multiply one's chances, is not playing: a licence obtained that way may be revoked.

Your data

The game asks for nothing about you, and keeps no list of players or of winners. To give each address one chance an hour and one licence, the licence server holds the addresses of the visitors who ask in its memory, for the day of the game only. Like any web server, it logs each request it receives, with the address it came from.
