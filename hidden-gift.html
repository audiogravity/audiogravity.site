Audiogravity · Christmas 2026

The hidden gift: the rules

The game

On 24 December 2026, five gifts hide on audiogravity.app. Each one is a lifetime Audiogravity licence for whoever finds it.

Who runs it

Alexandre Di Marco, publisher of Audiogravity at audiogravity.app. For anything about the game: contact@audiogravity.app.

When

From midnight to midnight on 24 December 2026, Paris time (UTC+1). The page dresses up for Christmas on 24 and 25 December by your own clock, so elsewhere you can play while one of those days meets Paris's 24th: in New York on the 24th from midnight to 18:00, in Tokyo from 08:00 on the 24th to 08:00 on the 25th.

How it works

  1. Through the day, the five licences wake one at a time, each at a moment drawn at random and kept secret by the Audiogravity licence server: one moment in each fifth of the day.
  2. The first visitor to open audiogravity.app after a licence wakes is shown a gold gift, somewhere on the page. Nobody else sees it.
  3. Open it, with a click or a tap, within twenty minutes, and the page gives you the licence key. If you do not, it hides again, and the next visitor has their chance.

Through the day, the banner at the top of the page says how many licences are left to win. Those nobody has found by midnight are not awarded.

The prize

A lifetime licence, the same as a bought one: Audiogravity Pro on one installation, every 1.x version included, with no end date. It has no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

Using your key

In Audiogravity, open the Admin tab, tap LICENSE KEY at the top of the licence panel and paste the key, then tap CHECK KEY and ACTIVATE THIS MACHINE. The key works once: the first box to activate it keeps it, so keep it to yourself. It does not expire: with no box yet, it will wait for you. The page you found it on keeps it on that device.

Who can play, and how often

Anyone, free of charge: no purchase, no account, no email address. Each internet address has one chance an hour, and can win one licence.

Fair play

Asking with automated requests, or through many addresses to multiply one's chances, is not playing: a licence obtained that way may be revoked.

Your data

The game asks for nothing about you, and keeps no list of players or of winners. To give each address one chance an hour and one licence, the licence server holds the addresses of the visitors who ask in its memory, for the day of the game only. Like any web server, it logs each request it receives, with the address it came from.
